# Akaru.fr Website Analysis

## 1. Initial Observations (Homepage)

The Akaru.fr homepage presents a clean, modern, and visually engaging design. The layout is divided into two main vertical sections. The left section features the company logo "AKARU" in a large, bold, sans-serif font, followed by a brief mission statement: "With a focus on precision expertise, our Lyon web agency brings together daring innovation and a meticulous eye for detail to deliver exceptional websites and designs." Below this, there's a horizontal line with social media icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook) that appear to be interactive.

The right section of the homepage is dynamic, showcasing various project visuals. These visuals appear to be part of a rotating or interactive display, hinting at animations and transitions. The overall color palette is muted with a light background, allowing the project visuals to stand out. The navigation links (Projects, Expertise, Agency, Contact) are subtly placed at the top, suggesting a minimalist approach to the header.

### UI/UX Design - Homepage:

*   **Layout:** Split-screen, vertical division, creating a clear visual hierarchy.
*   **Typography:** Large, bold sans-serif for headings and logo, smaller, legible sans-serif for body text.
*   **Color Palette:** Predominantly light background with contrasting dark text. Project visuals introduce a variety of colors.
*   **Navigation:** Minimalist top navigation, likely with hover effects or interactive states.
*   **Visuals:** High-quality project images/videos are central to the design, emphasizing the agency's work.
*   **Interactivity:** Social media icons at the bottom suggest external links. The dynamic right section implies interactive elements for project showcases.

## 8. Detailed UI/UX Analysis and Element Breakdown

This section provides a more granular analysis of specific UI/UX elements, their format, function, and behavior across the Akaru.fr website.

### 8.1. Global Elements

#### 8.1.1. Navigation (Top Right)
- **Format:** Minimalist, text-based links (`Projects`, `Expertise`, `Agency`, `Contact`) with a `MENU` button. The `MENU` button often has a small, pulsating dot next to it, indicating interactivity or new content.
- **Function:** Provides primary navigation to key sections of the website.
- **Behavior:**
  - **Hover:** Links likely change color or have a subtle underline animation on hover.
  - **Click:** Navigates to the respective page. The `MENU` button likely triggers an overlay or sidebar navigation.
  - **Responsiveness:** On smaller screens, the text links might collapse into a hamburger menu icon, which then expands to reveal the navigation options.

#### 8.1.2. Logo (Top Left)
- **Format:** Large, bold, sans-serif text "AKARU".
- **Function:** Brand identification and clickable link to the homepage.
- **Behavior:**
  - **Click:** Always returns the user to the homepage.
  - **Responsiveness:** Scales down in size on smaller screens to fit the viewport, maintaining its prominence.

#### 8.1.3. Social Media Icons (Footer/Homepage Bottom)
- **Format:** Small, monochromatic icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook) arranged horizontally, often with a thin line separating them.
- **Function:** Links to Akaru's social media profiles.
- **Behavior:**
  - **Hover:** Icons likely change color, scale slightly, or have a subtle glow effect.
  - **Click:** Opens the respective social media page in a new tab.

#### 8.1.4. Typography
- **Headings (H1, H2, etc.):** Large, bold, sans-serif fonts (e.g., a custom font similar to Monument Extended or similar impactful display sans-serif). Used for main titles, section headings, and key messages. Often uppercase.
- **Body Text:** Clean, highly legible sans-serif fonts (e.g., Open Sans, Lato, or similar). Used for paragraphs, descriptions, and detailed information. Good line height and letter spacing for readability.
- **Numbers:** Often styled with a larger font size and bold weight, sometimes with a unique color, to draw attention to statistics or sequential information.
- **Function:** Establishes visual hierarchy, conveys brand personality, and ensures readability.
- **Behavior:** Consistent across the site, maintaining brand identity. Font sizes adapt responsively to screen size.

#### 8.1.5. Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Light, off-white or very light grey (#F5F5F5 or similar) for a clean, minimalist feel.
- **Accent Colors:** Used sparingly for interactive elements, highlights, or to differentiate sections. These include muted tones like sage green, dusty rose, lavender, and soft blue, often used as background colors for horizontally scrolling sections.
- **Text Color:** Dark grey or black for primary text (#333333 or #000000) for high contrast.
- **Function:** Creates a sophisticated, modern, and visually appealing aesthetic. Differentiates content sections.
- **Behavior:** Colors are consistently applied. Background colors of sections change smoothly during horizontal transitions.

### 8.2. Homepage Specific Elements

#### 8.2.1. Dynamic Project Showcase (Right Panel)
- **Format:** A large, responsive container displaying high-quality images or video snippets of projects. Often includes a project title, year, and a brief description. Numbers (e.g., 01, 02, 03) are used to indicate the current project in the sequence.
- **Function:** Visually showcases Akaru's portfolio and capabilities. Acts as a primary engagement point.
- **Behavior:**
  - **Auto-play/Loop:** Projects transition automatically with a smooth horizontal slide or fade animation.
  - **Manual Navigation:** Small navigation dots or arrows might be present to allow users to manually cycle through projects.
  - **Parallax:** As the user scrolls vertically, the right panel might scroll at a different speed or have subtle shifts, creating a parallax effect against the fixed left panel.
  - **Hover:** Project visuals might pause or show additional information on hover.

#### 8.2.2. Fixed Left Panel (Homepage)
- **Format:** Contains the main logo, mission statement, and sometimes social media links. Its content remains static while the right panel scrolls.
- **Function:** Provides persistent brand identity and core messaging.
- **Behavior:** Remains fixed in position, creating a visual anchor and contrast to the dynamic right panel.

### 8.7. General Animations and Transitions

#### 8.7.1. Page Load Animations
- **Behavior:** Elements often fade in or slide up from the bottom of the screen upon initial page load, creating a smooth and engaging entry.

#### 8.7.2. Scroll-Triggered Animations
- **Behavior:** Many elements (text blocks, images, cards) animate into view as they enter the viewport. These can include:
  - **Fade-in:** Elements gradually become visible.
  - **Slide-up/down/left/right:** Elements slide into their final position.
  - **Scale:** Elements grow from a smaller size.
  - **Staggered reveals:** Multiple elements in a group (e.g., list items, grid cards) animate in one after another, creating a ripple effect.

#### 8.7.3. Smooth Scrolling
- **Behavior:** The website likely uses smooth scrolling, where vertical scroll actions are eased rather than jumping, contributing to a fluid user experience.

#### 8.7.4. Parallax Effects
- **Behavior:** Background images or elements move at a slower rate than foreground content during scrolling, creating a sense of depth. This is particularly noticeable on the homepage with the fixed left panel and scrolling right panel.

#### 8.7.5. Cursor Effects (Potential)
- **Behavior:** Some modern agency websites implement custom cursor effects (e.g., a larger, animated dot, or a cursor that changes shape/color over interactive elements). While not explicitly observed in detail, it's a common trend in this design style.

#### 8.7.6. Micro-interactions
- **Behavior:** Subtle animations on small interactive elements like buttons, links, and form fields (e.g., input field highlights, button presses, checkbox animations) provide immediate visual feedback to user actions.
